Branch: owner header and payload pop together; one-op token kill.
Previous receipt: arena_lifetime_20260904_tokens.txt.

What changed:
- cc_arena_owner_release: when the header sits right below its payload
  (they were minted together by one bump) and the payload ends the current
  slab, both pop in one CAS with no lock and nothing is listed. The next
  owner_new then finds no listed header and mints header + payload with
  one CAS again. A buried payload still becomes a hole with the header
  listed for rebirth (covered by arena_owner_token_smoke).
- cc_slice_gen_kill is one fetch_and instead of a load + CAS loop
  (cc_atomic_fetch_and added to every atomics branch).
- The string append row reserves its capacity on both sides so it measures
  the append, not the growth policy and first touch of 12 MiB. The inline
  append is 11 instructions; the remaining 2 ns over C is the owner-token
  check and the inline / heap branch.
