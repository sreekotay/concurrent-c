What changed:
- The "@string template (2 slots) on @scratch" row bound the product
  (`CCString t = @string(..., @scratch)`) and never released it, so after
  about 30 iterations every string was a malloc-backed overflow record held
  until the function returned. Its number was malloc plus overflow
  bookkeeping and page faults, and it moved between 360 and 1300 ns/op
  with heap state. The row is now the call-local shape
  (`consume(@string(..., @scratch))`) and is named accordingly.
- The lowerer reclaims a call-local scratch template around any consuming
  statement (call, assignment, UFCS call, `!>` unwrap), not only println:
  one checkpoint before the temp, one restore after the call. Before this
  change the call-local shape leaked the same way as the bound one.
- Contended rows vary run to run on this 4-core box (89 to 120 ns for the
  bump, 215 to 273 for bump plus release); the raw 4-thread CAS floor
  measured on the same box is 40 ns for one bump and 62 for bump plus
  release. The C twin's contended rows are glibc's thread-local cache and
  share nothing.
