What the numbers say:
- Bump allocation is unchanged on the shared path; the exclusive path pays
  about 1.5 ns for the active-chain and reuse-flag tests.
- A checkpoint cycle costs about 15 ns more than the snapshot it replaces,
  and a @scratch string-template shape about 10 ns more. In exchange a
  pre-checkpoint owner can grow during scratch, holes never disable a
  capture, and a restore never rewinds under a live object.
- Vec push keeps its inlined fast path (2.3 ns in the release build). The
  per-push token check is roughly 1 ns of that; it is what refuses a stale
  copy of the handle with spare capacity.
- Owner churn (create + destroy of a Vec or a heap String) costs about
  30 ns more: a header rebirth off the owner list plus a lock-free registry
  birth and kill. Header and payload come off the slab under one lock.
- The @scratch template row is dominated by template building, not by the
  checkpoint; the difference to the on-arena row is the checkpoint cycle
  plus the stack arena's smaller tail.
