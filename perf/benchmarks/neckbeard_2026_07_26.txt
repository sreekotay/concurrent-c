Throughput: a 1:1 runtime (pthread/go/zig) drains all 100 kidnappers

the baseline CC's promotion beats; read the 'Kidnappers Completed' column.

Tradeoffs:
  - condvar, Zig, and CC-chan all wake exactly one waiter; condvar/
    Zig pay a kernel futex round-trip per wake, CC-chan pays scheduler
    dispatch in user-space.
  - Go wakes one goroutine via its runtime's sudog queue (no kernel
    syscall for the wake path itself).
  - pipe row is NOT a competing baseline: it's the textbook herd case,
    reported to show what a naive 'wake via fd' design costs at N=1000.
