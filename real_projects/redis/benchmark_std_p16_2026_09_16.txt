4 workers measured like 1. Fiber parks under load: 7-24 per million requests, so the shard hold is not a cost.

CAVEAT on every "4-thread client" figure above: redis-benchmark 7.0.15 with --threads reports
elapsed time in 0.25 s ticks, so a 1.5M-request run "in 0.75 s" is anywhere in [1.5M, 3.0M) rps.
The orderings hold (same request count, different tick counts); the absolute values do not.
Long runs make the tick small: see the 8M-request robust capture below.
