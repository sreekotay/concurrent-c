=== Root cause of the earlier "gap" ===
Earlier benches passed CC_WORKERS=16 to "match" pigz -p 16. Wrong knob
equivalence: pigz's 16 threads mostly block in handoffs, so the kernel
packs them onto 10 cores cleanly; CC_WORKERS=16 makes 16 CPU-hungry
scheduler workers on 10 cores and the oversubscription cost ~4-8% wall.
sched_v2.c already documents this (~5% on pigz 100MB silesia).

Worker sweep (dict, best of 3, 200MB):
  workers=10  cap=32/48/64: 0.57 0.57 0.57
  workers=12  cap=32/48/64: 0.58 0.57 0.57
  workers=16  cap=32/48/64: 0.60 0.59 0.58
  workers=20  cap=32/48/64: 0.60 0.59 0.59
PIGZ_CAP is flat; CC_WORKERS above core count is the whole effect.

=== Rule for future benches ===
Do not set CC_WORKERS. The runtime default (1x core count) is correct
for CPU-bound work; "match pigz -p N" is the wrong mental model.
